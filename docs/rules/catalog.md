## Texts

### CA-FIN-001 Cost-allocation tags on container resources

Container platform resources carry the tags that let their cost be allocated to a cost centre, an owner and an environment.

Remediation: Apply the costCenter, owner and environment tags (or inherit them through Azure Policy) so cost can be allocated.

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

### CA-FIN-002 Estate tag coverage at or above 80 percent

At least 80 percent of exported resources carry a cost-centre tag, so allocation reports cover most of the spend; with cost evidence, at most 20 percent of month-to-date spend is untagged.

Remediation: Enforce a cost-centre tag with Azure Policy (inherit from resource group or subscription) and remediate existing resources.

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

### CA-FIN-003 Budgets exist for the scope

The scope has at least one budget so spend is compared with a plan and alerts fire before it is exceeded.

Remediation: Create a budget at subscription or resource-group scope with alerts at 80 and 100 percent.

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

### CA-FIN-004 Non-production container apps scale to zero

Container apps in non-production environments allow zero minimum replicas so idle time costs nothing.

Remediation: Set the scale rule's minimum replicas to 0 for development and test apps.

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

### CA-FIN-005 Non-production AKS clusters use the Free tier

AKS clusters tagged as non-production use the Free control-plane tier rather than paying for an uptime SLA they do not need.

Remediation: Switch development and test clusters to the Free tier; keep Standard or Premium for production.

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

### CA-FIN-006 Premium registry tier is justified

A Premium container registry uses a Premium-only capability such as private link; otherwise the tier is reviewed.

Remediation: Premium without private link or geo-replication: review the tier, or add the private endpoint the tier was chosen for.

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.

### CA-FIN-007 An alerting signal exists in the estate

Attribution: Paraphrased from the FinOps Foundation Framework (finops.org); not affiliated. Retrieved 2026-10-10.
